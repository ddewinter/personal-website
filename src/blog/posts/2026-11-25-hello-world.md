I've built a lot of things over the last 15 years: products, teams, a company or two, and more tutorials than I can count. What I haven't done is write much of it down. This blog is where I start.

## What I'll write about

Mostly the things I'm working through right now:

- **Putting AI to work.** Not the hype, but the specific workflows that give people hours back each week, and the ones that didn't pan out.
- **Building products.** Lessons from RowHero, from leading product teams, and from shipping on a small budget.
- **Teaching.** What I've learned about explaining technical things so they stick.

## Why in public

Writing forces me to finish a thought. If I can't explain something plainly, I probably don't understand it yet. Sharing it also means someone smarter than me might reply and tell me what I got wrong, which is the fastest way to learn.

> The best way to learn something is to try to teach it.

If something here is useful to you, or you think I've missed the mark, [I'd like to hear about it](/contact/).
